# EU AI Act Risk Classification Tool

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.x-blue)
![Source](https://img.shields.io/badge/Source-EUR--Lex%202024%2F1689-navy)

A Python tool that classifies AI systems against the four risk tiers of EU Regulation 2024/1689.

---

## Risk Tiers

| Tier | Article |
|------|---------|
| 🔴 Unacceptable Risk | Article 5 |
| 🟠 High Risk | Article 6 + Annex III |
| 🟡 Limited Risk | Article 50 |
| 🟢 Minimal Risk | Recital 48 |

---

## How to Use

Open `eu_ai_act_classifier.ipynb` in Jupyter Notebook and run all cells in order.
